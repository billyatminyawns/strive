STRIVE

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Strive is an app where fans hear from athletes — voice drops, and questions answered back in the athlete's voice. We keep the information we collect to the minimum needed to run it. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your information.

What we collect

  • Account basics. A random account identifier created when you join with an invite code, and the first name you choose to share (optional).
  • Your interests. The topics you pick, so suggestions fit what you care about.
  • Your questions. The questions you send an athlete, whether you saved a reply, and which drops you've listened to.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, a device token that lets us tell you when an athlete answers or posts a new drop.
  • Athletes only. If you use the athlete studio: recordings you choose to save, their transcripts, and your approvals and edits.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. Recordings are only kept if you tap save; transcription happens on your iPhone when your device supports it.

How we use it

Only to run Strive: to deliver your questions to the athlete, return their approved replies, remember what you saved, count listens so athletes know what's resonating, and send the notifications you asked for.

AI and the athlete's voice

Replies and drops are spoken with an AI model of the athlete's voice, made with the athlete's permission. Every word you hear was written or approved by the athlete before it reached you, and it's labelled as their AI voice wherever it plays. When the athlete has turned it on, an AI system may draft a suggested reply to your question for the athlete to review, edit, or discard — nothing is sent without their approval.

Who helps us run it

  • Cloudflare hosts our servers and database.
  • Anthropic processes question text to draft suggested replies for the athlete (only when drafting is enabled).
  • Fish Audio turns athlete-approved text into audio in the athlete's AI voice.
  • Apple delivers push notifications if you enable them.

These providers process information on our behalf to provide their service to us, and not for their own advertising.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can change your name and interests anytime in the app. To delete your account, open You → Delete account. That permanently removes your profile, your questions, your saved replies, and your listening history from Strive. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they're overwritten.

Children

Strive isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: see Strive support.
