STRIVE

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Strive is an app where fans hear from athletes — voice drops, and questions answered back in the athlete's voice. We keep the information we collect to the minimum needed to run it. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your information.

What we collect

  • Account basics. A random account identifier created when you join with an invite code, and the first name you choose to share (optional).
  • Sign-in details (optional). If you save your account, the email address you sign in with — and if you choose Google, the account ID and email address Google shares with us. We never see your Google password, and we don't get your contacts.
  • Your interests. The topics you pick, so suggestions fit what you care about.
  • Your questions. The questions you send an athlete, whether you saved a reply, and which drops you've listened to.
  • Notifications. The in-app alerts we keep for you when an athlete answers or posts a new drop.
  • Athletes only. If you use the athlete studio: recordings you choose to save, their transcripts, and your approvals and edits.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we don't use analytics or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps your sign-in and a copy of what you've loaded so the app opens quickly; signing out or deleting your account clears them. Studio recordings are only kept if the athlete taps save. While an athlete records, live transcription uses the browser's built-in speech recognition, which the browser's maker may process (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari).

How we use it

Only to run Strive: to sign you in (including emailing the sign-in codes you ask for — we don't send newsletters or marketing email), to deliver your questions to the athlete, return their approved replies, remember what you saved, count listens so athletes know what's resonating, and send the notifications you asked for.

AI and the athlete's voice

Replies and drops are spoken with an AI model of the athlete's voice, made with the athlete's permission, and labelled as their AI voice wherever it plays. Replies are written or approved by the athlete before they reach you. When drafting is on, an AI system may suggest a reply for the athlete to review, edit, or discard. If an athlete chooses to turn on their AI Coach, it may answer some questions on its own using only the athlete's verified public record; those answers are labelled AI Coach, show their sources, and the athlete can retract them.

Who helps us run it

  • Cloudflare hosts our servers and database.
  • GitHub serves the app's web pages, and Google Fonts serves its typeface; like any web host, they see your IP address when pages load.
  • Google confirms who you are if you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in codes you request by email.
  • Anthropic processes question text to draft suggested replies for the athlete, and to answer when an athlete has turned on their AI Coach.
  • Fish Audio turns athlete-approved text into audio in the athlete's AI voice.

These providers process information on our behalf to provide their service to us, and not for their own advertising.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can change your name and interests anytime in the app. To delete your account, open You → Delete my account. That permanently removes your profile, your questions, your saved replies, your listening history, and your sign-in details from Strive. You can also remove a sign-in method on its own under You → Your account. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they're overwritten.

Children

Strive isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: see Strive support.
