Help with the Strive app

How do I get in?

Strive is invite-only while the first athletes build their rooms. Enter the invite code an athlete shared with you on the welcome screen.

Is that really the athlete's voice?

It's an AI model of their voice, made with their permission, and it's labelled as their AI voice wherever it plays. Replies are written or approved by the athlete. If an athlete turns on their AI Coach, some answers come from it instead — they're marked AI Coach, drawn only from the athlete's public record, with the sources shown.

Why didn't my question get an answer?

Athletes answer what they can speak to best, and some topics — medical, betting and legal questions — always get a polite pass. Athletes can also pause new questions while they're away.

Where do replies show up?

In Ask, right under your question, and under the bell on Home — the Home tab shows a badge when something's new. Strive doesn't send push notifications or email yet.

How do I put Strive on my phone?

Open Strive in your phone's browser. iPhone: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen). It then opens full-screen like any other app.

On iPhone, the Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari. Easiest: add Strive to your Home Screen first and join from there. Already joined in Safari? Save your seat first (below), then sign in inside the Home Screen app.

How do I get back in on another phone?

Save your seat: open You → Your account and add an email address (we send you a 6-digit code) or your Google account. Then on any phone — or in the Home Screen app — tap “Already joined? Sign in”. Your questions and saved replies come with you. If you used Google, an email code sent to that same address works too.

How do I delete my account?

Open You → Delete my account. Your profile, questions, saved replies and listening history are permanently removed.

I'm an athlete — how do I sign in?

Tap "I'm an athlete — sign in to my studio" on the welcome screen and enter the studio key from the Strive team. After that, set up Google or email sign-in in Studio settings so you never have to paste the key again.
